## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet grandeur of an architectural studio arch, bespoke celebratory moments, and haute-couture gift curation into an immersive digital medium. It pairs tactile editorial elegance with contemporary restraint.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic unites **Architectural Minimalism** with **Warm Editorial Luxury**. It draws inspiration from immaculate interior arches, tactile plaster textures, brushed gold metalwork, and delicate floral botanical accents. The visual canvas emphasizes serene expanses of warm ivory, sculpted typographic hierarchies, and luminous highlights rather than heavy artificial chrome.

### Brand Persona & Mood
- **Luminous & Serene:** Generous negative space, whisper-soft gradients, and glowing champagne undertones that evoke sunlight entering an immaculate white studio gallery.
- **Bespoke & Discerning:** Precision typography, bespoke gold trims, and editorial layout proportions that reflect thoughtful curation and high-touch craftsmanship.
- **Graceful & Celebratory:** Delicate rose-petal flushes and refined warm metallics evoke timeless joy, romantic milestones, and refined celebrations without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean, luminous canvas anchored by radiant metallics, rich graphite ink, and warm romantic flushes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C8A252`) & Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** Warm polished gold and royal champagne brass. Applied with calculated restraint to calligraphic accents, delicate dividers, active states, key interactive badges, and jewel-like iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#E8A598`):** Petal blush and rose de mai. Used selectively for secondary interactive states, celebratory tags, promotional micro-moments, and soft decorative glows.
- **Neutral (`#1E1E20`):** Polished graphite and deep charcoal. Replaces harsh true black to preserve warmth while guaranteeing razor-sharp legibility across all editorial typography.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background layers utilize `#FDFBF7` (Immaculate Ivory), `#F9F6F0` (Champagne Cream), and `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Studio Light) to simulate natural daylight on smooth architectural plaster.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast transitional elegance of **Playfair Display** with the pristine geometric neutrality of **Manrope**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements:** Set Playfair Display with generous leading to convey literary luxury and celebratory prestige. Headlines must never be crowded; grant them vertical breathing room against cream backgrounds.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Set Manrope uppercase for badges, category markers, and navigation triggers, styled with extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) to evoke physical atelier hallmarks and luxury perfume packaging.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives:** Manrope regular weights ensure effortless reading across bespoke gift descriptions and celebration itineraries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model takes inspiration from high-fashion lookbooks and modern architectural spaces, avoiding dense grid crowding in favor of sweeping margins and deliberate pauses.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum content container of 1320px for desktop. For mobile devices, the system transitions to a 4-column structure with edge-to-edge breathing room.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Editorial sections are divided by broad whitespace bands (`space-xl` scaled dynamically) or hairline metallic rules. Content flows rhythmically: large portrait imagery is paired with off-center typographic vignettes to echo physical art monographs.
- **Architectural Arch Layouts:** Key focal cards, promotional hero stages, and gallery frames utilize tall vertical aspect ratios (e.g., 4:5 or 2:3) framed by architectural top-curved outlines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews dark drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and tonal layering that mimics warm sunlight falling across sculpted gallery walls.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Pristine `#FDFBF7` matte ivory plaster foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards and floating palettes with whisper-thin borders: `1px solid rgba(200, 162, 82, 0.18)` (warm gold filament).
- **Tier 2 (Floating Popovers & Modals):** Luminous ambient blur. A wide multi-layered shadow tinted with warm gold ochre: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(200, 162, 82, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 30, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Frosted Veil (Overlays):** Translucent backdrop blurs (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), evoking sheer silk organza or fluted glass.

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, tailoring subtle softened angles with structural curves to reflect clean stonework and atelier architecture.

### Edge Radii & Arches
- **Micro & Standard Elements:** Input fields, interactive pills, and buttons leverage a softened, restrained corner radius (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`), keeping contours sharp and tailored.
- **Signature Arch Geometry:** Hero display elements, image showcases, and featured product enclosures apply a distinctive continuous arch contour: upper corners rounded at `12rem` or fully semi-circular (`rounded-t-full`), while maintaining clean, square lower footings (`rounded-b-none`).
- **Divider Filaments:** Hairline rules (`1px`) accented with central diamond or four-point star terminals inspired by classic fine jewelry hallmarks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxe Button:** Solid rich gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C8A252 100%)`) with white or dark graphite typography (`label-lg`), subtle letter-spacing, and an inner hairline sheen. Hover states invoke a delicate gold bloom (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Architectural Button:** Ghost style with a pristine white or transparent body, framed by a `1px solid #C8A252` border, with graphite text that transitions to warm gold on hover.
- **Text Link Buttons:** Playfair or Manrope with an animated hairline underline expanding outward from the center.

### Cards & Gift Tiles
- **The Atelier Arch Card:** Framed with an arched top, ivory or white ground, and a delicate `1px` border in champagne gold. Photography fills the arch naturally, bordered below by Playfair product headlines, blush floral tags, and pricing in graphite.
- **Minimalist Floating Card:** Square with `rounded-sm` edges, resting on subtle champagne glow shadows that intensify softly on pointer hover.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Generous padding with warm ivory fills (`#F9F6F0`). Borders rest at `1px solid rgba(30, 30, 32, 0.12)` and transition on focus to `1px solid #C8A252` accompanied by a soft champagne focus ring. Placeholder text is rendered in muted warm charcoal.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist custom circular indicators. Selected states display an inner gold jewel dot or a clean fine-line tick against a warm blush background.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Tags:** Pill or architectural shapes featuring delicate blush backgrounds (`rgba(232, 165, 152, 0.15)`), rose-tinted border filaments, and small uppercase Manrope typography (`label-sm`). Used for seasonal flowers, bespoke gift tiers, and celebratory themes.
